2. What data we process, and why

Traeller is a bird-song identification and collection game. We keep data to a minimum.

DataWhenPurposeLegal basis (GDPR Art. 6)

Microphone / audio: birdsong is recorded and analysed on your device. The raw audio is never uploaded and is discarded after identification — only the resulting species + the small metrics above are stored.

We do not use advertising, analytics/tracking SDKs, or profiling, and we do not sell or share your data for marketing.

3. Who processes data on our behalf

  • Supabase (database, authentication, hosting) — data is stored in the Central EU (Frankfurt) region, within the EU/EEA.
  • Resend — sends the one-time login codes to your email address.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account and catches until you delete your account. You can delete it at any time in Settings → Delete account, which permanently removes your profile and all your catches. Backups are rotated and purged within 7 days.

5. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access (Art. 15), rectify (16), erase (17), restrict (18), port (20), and object (21) to the processing of your data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Erasure is self-service via Settings → Delete account; for anything else, contact kroenert.mx@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Germany, your state’s Datenschutzbehörde).

6. Children

Traeller is not directed at children under 12. We do not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date reflects the latest version. Material changes will be surfaced in the app.
