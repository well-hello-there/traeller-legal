Delete your Traeller account & data

This page explains how to delete your account and all associated data in Traeller (also listed as Traeller), developed by Max Krönert. There are two ways to do it.

1. In the app (instant)

Open Traeller → Settings (gear icon) → Delete account, then confirm. Your account and all your data are removed immediately and permanently.

2. By email (e.g. if you've uninstalled the app)

Send an email to traeller.app@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We verify the request and delete your account within 30 days (usually much sooner), then confirm by reply.

What gets deleted

Your account is erased along with all data linked to it: your email address, display name, and every bird you've caught (your collection, points and leaderboard entries). The deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. We do not retain personal data after deletion.

Auf Deutsch

Konto und Daten löschen. In der App: Einstellungen (Zahnrad) → Konto löschen → bestätigen (sofort und dauerhaft). Falls du die App bereits deinstalliert hast, schreib uns an traeller.app@gmail.com von deiner Anmelde-E-Mail-Adresse mit dem Betreff „Konto löschen“ — wir löschen dein Konto samt aller Daten (E-Mail, Anzeigename und alle Fänge) innerhalb von 30 Tagen dauerhaft und bestätigen dies per Antwort. Es werden keine personenbezogenen Daten aufbewahrt.
