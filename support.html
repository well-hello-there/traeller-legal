Traeller — Support

Traeller identifies the birds around you by their song and lets you collect them in your Birdex. Need help, found a bug, or have feedback?

Common questions

The app identified a bird but it didn't count. A catch only scores when the species is expected in your region, and scoring currently covers Europe. Identification still works everywhere; regional scoring is expanding.

Nothing was recognised. Get closer, aim the phone toward the bird, and avoid wind and traffic. Traeller needs to hear the same species clearly across the recording.

Delete my account/data. In the app: Settings (gear icon) → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all your catches. Uninstalled the app? See Delete your account & data to request it by email.
